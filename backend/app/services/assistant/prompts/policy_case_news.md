This asks about a court case, ruling, release, announcement or news item.
- Lead with a two-to-three-sentence summary: what was decided or announced, by whom, and when.
- Then a short "Why it matters" part (two to four bullets) for the reader - who is affected and what changes.
- If you cannot find the item, say so plainly and say what you searched for; do not guess what it says.
- Aim for about 150-250 words.
